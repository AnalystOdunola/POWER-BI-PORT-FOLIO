# 📱 TSA 2023 Gadgets Sales Analysis Dashboard

## Overview

This Power BI dashboard analyzes gadget sales performance across brands, product categories, customer segments, regions, and income levels. The dashboard provides insights into revenue generation, profitability, customer behavior, and sales trends to support business decision-making.

## Business Problem

Businesses selling consumer electronics need to understand which brands, customer segments, and regions generate the most revenue and profit. Without a centralized reporting system, identifying growth opportunities and high-performing products becomes difficult.

The goal of this project was to build an interactive dashboard that answers key questions:

* Which brands generate the highest profit?
* Which product categories contribute the most revenue?
* Which customer segments are most profitable?
* How does revenue vary across regions?
* What sales trends exist over time?
